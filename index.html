About

I build backend systems and AI automation for healthcare. My background spans bioengineering and computer science, and I like problems where messy real-world data has to become something people can rely on.

I have more than seven years of experience as a software engineer, most recently at Infinitus and before that at Amazon. My work centers on scalable backend services, LLM-based voice agents, and data resolution engines where precision matters.

I also mentor interns and new hires, interview candidates, and help teams improve how they run operations. I’m looking for Senior SDE roles in health-tech, with a longer-term path toward engineering management.
